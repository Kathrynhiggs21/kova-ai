# V8 Visual Review Checklist

Review the contact sheet and individual masters for:

- accurate subject and habitat
- irregular 8–18% pop-color exposure rather than a uniform border
- asymmetrical natural framing
- title and lower-panel material match
- readable text contrast
- correct identifier type
- one canonical ID only
- integrated variable plaque
- crop safety and unobstructed anatomy
- readability at app and thumbnail sizes

Mock art is checked for layout and packaging only. Gemini art also requires anatomy, species, and habitat review.
